EchoForge

M.S. research · in progress

Generates room impulse responses from text prompts in real time, combining signal processing with generative models. The goal is to give AI agents a sense of the acoustic space around them.

Individualized headphone equalization

FAPESP fellowship · 2020–2021

Personalized headphone equalization for binaural (3D) audio, using regularized inverse filters with automatic parameter selection to avoid audible peaks. Advised by Prof. Bruno Sanches Masiero at UNICAMP. Grant 20/10195-7.
